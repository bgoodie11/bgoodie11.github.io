A FEEDBACK LOOP FOR PERSONAL AI AGENTS

Recommendations that learn.

Your agent suggests a spot. You actually go. It follows up, records what you thought, and turns the real story into a public review — only with your approval. Every loop makes the next recommendation sharper.

THE LOOP

  1. 01 · RECOMMENDYour agent suggests a place, product, or service — recorded with the why, so there's something to follow up on.
  2. 02 · EXPERIENCEYou go live your life. The agent checks back in and records how it actually went: rating, what landed, what didn't.
  3. 03 · REVIEWYour genuine feedback becomes a public review draft. Nothing publishes until you approve the exact text and stars.
  4. 04 · LEARNEvery experience feeds the next suggestion. The loop compounds — recommendations grounded in your real taste.

GROUND RULES

  • Only your facts. Ratings, opinions, and details come from you — never invented, never embellished.
  • Your approval, every time. No public review goes out without you signing off on the exact words.
  • Neutral follow-ups. Good nights and bad nights get the same nudge. No cherry-picking five-star moments.
  • Your data, your call. Export or delete everything whenever you want.
